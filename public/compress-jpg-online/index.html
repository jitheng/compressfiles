How to compress a JPG image online

  1. Click "Compress Image" and select your JPG or JPEG file (up to 50 MB)
  2. Choose a compression level: Low (~70% smaller), Medium (~85% smaller), or High (~90% smaller)
  3. Download your compressed JPG instantly — no sign-up required

Understanding JPEG compression quality levels

  • Low (85% quality) — The quality loss is nearly invisible at normal screen sizes. Ideal for archiving, professional use, and when quality matters most.
  • Medium (60% quality) — Excellent for web pages, social media, and email. Sharp colours, clear text, significantly smaller file.
  • High (35% quality) — Maximum reduction for when file size is the priority. Some visible softness on detailed areas.

Why choose browser-based JPG compression?

When you upload a JPEG to a server for compression, you're handing your photo to a third party. CompressFiles never receives your image. Compression is performed locally using the browser's Canvas API — the same technology used by professional image editing tools. The result is equivalent quality to desktop software like Photoshop at the same quality setting, with zero privacy risk.
