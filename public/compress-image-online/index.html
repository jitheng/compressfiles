Why compress images in your browser?

Most online image compressors upload your photos to a remote server for processing. CompressFiles is different — compression runs 100% locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your image never leaves your device. This makes it the most private image compressor available, and it works even without an internet connection after the page loads.

Compression levels explained

  • Low — 85% JPEG quality, ~70% file size reduction. Virtually indistinguishable from the original at normal screen sizes.
  • Medium — 60% JPEG quality, ~85% reduction. Ideal for web, email, and social media sharing.
  • High — 35% JPEG quality, ~90% reduction. Best when file size is critical and some quality loss is acceptable.
